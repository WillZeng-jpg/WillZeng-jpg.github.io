SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT / LEARNING CASE

International Sourcing Cost Comparison

Hypothetical educational case with simulated inputs. Prepared with AI assistance for Hao Zeng’s review and learning. This is not employment experience, client work, or evidence of implemented business results. All conclusions are model outputs, not observed outcomes.

Business question

A hypothetical US importer needs 2,000 reusable bottles. Which of three fictional suppliers offers the best balance of landed cost and delivery time?

Scenario and inputs

All quotes are in USD. Quality and capacity are assumed equal, and lead times are deterministic calendar days. Duty percentages are invented teaching inputs, not actual tariff rates for any country or product. Duty is applied only to purchase value in this simplified model. Freight covers transportation to the receiving warehouse; handling is a separate administrative charge.

Method

Landed cost = quantity × unit price + freight + (quantity × unit price × assumed duty rate) + handling.

Supplier A: 2,000 × $8 + $2,400 + $800 + $600 = $19,800.

Supplier B: $17,000 + $1,700 + $510 + $600 = $19,810. Supplier C: $18,400 + $900 + $0 + $400 = $19,700.

Findings and sensitivity

Supplier C: $9.85 per unit · 12-day lead time

C has the highest quoted unit price but the lowest modeled landed cost: $100 below A and $110 below B. It also has the shortest assumed lead time.

If C’s freight rises by $100, C ties A at $19,800. If it rises by 20% ($180), C totals $19,880, so A becomes the cost minimum by $80. A 30-day delivery requirement would make A ineligible; among B and C, C can absorb a $110 freight increase before tying B.

Recommendation

Select C under the base assumptions. Because its cost advantage is small, verify quotes, supplier qualification, and delivery reliability before making a real purchasing decision. No purchase was made.

Limitations

The model omits exchange-rate exposure, financing, taxes beyond the assumed duty, quality variation, and supplier capacity constraints. It is a simplified comparison, not a customs estimate.

Skills demonstrated by the case

Landed-cost modeling, supplier comparison, scenario analysis, and recommendation writing.
